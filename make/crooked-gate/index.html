The Project

Crooked Gate Seasonings.

Crooked Gate already had the part that mattered: a real product.

The job was turning that into something customers could actually discover, understand, shop and buy.

That meant building the customer-facing brand presentation, product structure, recipe content, responsive website, cart system, fulfillment flow and Square checkout around it.

What Got Built

More Than A Website.

Brand System

Product First.

Built the customer-facing visual system around the seasoning labels instead of burying them behind generic web design.

Web Design

Built Around The Product.

Responsive pages, product discovery, recipes and a shopping experience designed to keep buying simple.

E-Commerce

The Pantry.

A custom cart system where customers can choose product sizes and add seasonings without unnecessary navigation.

Checkout

Square Integration.

Connected the storefront to Square checkout with product data, fulfillment logic, shipping and payment flow.

Fulfillment

Pickup Or Shipping.

Local pickup and California shipping built into the purchase flow without turning the website into a form factory.

Content

Recipes With A Job.

Recipes and product pages designed to show how the seasonings actually fit into everyday cooking.

The Point

Make It Useful.

A brand is not a logo.

A website is not a homepage.

And a store is not finished because somebody added a checkout button.

The interesting part is figuring out how all of it works together.
