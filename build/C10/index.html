Build · 1972 Chevy C10

1972 C10

Unfucking This Death Trap.

Somebody had already “built” this truck. My job was to make sure it stopped trying to kill its owner—or anybody else on the road.

01 · Let's See What We're Working With

Well, That's Not Supposed To Be Loose.

The truck had already been modified before it came to me.

Unfortunately, whoever did the work apparently considered properly torquing the rear-end hardware optional.

Loose hardware in the rear suspension isn't a cosmetic problem. This is the kind of shit that needs to be found before somebody discovers it at highway speed.

02 · Fix The Actual Problem

Adding Bags.

The truck had been lowered badly enough that the frame was contacting the axle housing.

The goal wasn't to throw expensive parts at it or rebuild the entire suspension just because I could. The goal was to solve the problem in a way that made sense for the owner.

Airbags were the most budget-friendly solution. They corrected the clearance problem and gave the truck the additional support it needed to still be useful for towing.

The mounts didn't exist for what I needed to do. So I made them.

Fabrication · Suspension

Make What You Need.

Custom-fabricated airbag mounts, installed to correct the axle-to-frame interference without turning the owner's budget into another casualty.
